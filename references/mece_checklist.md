# MECE Category Checklist — SEBI India Compliance Research v2.0
*Read at Phase 3, BEFORE broad searching. Coverage is by construction: a category not
tracked here cannot be silently missing.*

Usage: mark each category covered / not covered / N/A as research progresses.
A "not covered" after research completes is a gap to investigate; an N/A needs a
one-line reason. For non-LODR domains, build the equivalent deductive set per
SKILL.md Part 2 step 3.

## The 19 Categories (listed-company / LODR instantiation)

| # | Category | Primary Regulation | Commonly Missed Items |
|---|----------|-------------------|----------------------|
| 1 | Financial Results | LODR Reg 33, 47, 32 | Statement on Impact of Audit Qualifications (Form A/B); CEO/CFO certificate format; newspaper advertisement within 48 hours |
| 2 | Shareholding Pattern | LODR Reg 31 | Post-listing pattern (1 WD prior to listing); 10-day filing after >2% capital restructuring; Reg 31(1)(a) vs 31(1)(b) sub-cases |
| 3 | Corporate Governance | LODR Reg 27, 34, 24A, 40, 74 | Reg 7(3) half-yearly electronic transfer facility certificate; Reg 40(9) is ANNUAL not half-yearly; material-subsidiary governance framework |
| 4 | Related Party Transactions | LODR Reg 23 | RPT ratification conditions; omnibus approval for subsidiaries; PSC exemption scope |
| 5 | Board Meetings | LODR Reg 29, 30(6) | 30-min rule during market hours vs 3-hr rule after hours — state both |
| 6 | Material Events — Deemed (Sch III Part A) | LODR Reg 30 + Sch III Part A | Forensic audit initiation; fraud by promoter even if unrelated to the listed entity; SR equity share changes |
| 7 | Material Events — Policy-based (Sch III Part B) | LODR Reg 30 + Sch III Part B | Orders/contracts above materiality; new business commencement; capacity changes |
| 8 | AGM / EGM / Voting | LODR Reg 44, 30 | Voting results timeline — verify against current text (48 hours vs 2 trading days has changed by amendment) |
| 9 | Dividends / Record Date / Ex-Date | LODR Reg 42, 43 | Ex-date intimation (separate from record date); minimum 5-day gap between record dates; payment within 30 days |
| 10 | Insider Trading (PIT) | SEBI PIT Regulations 2015 | Initial disclosure 7 DAYS (not 30 — common error); annual disclosure by April 30; SDD 8-year preservation |
| 11 | SAST — Substantial Acquisition | SEBI SAST Regulations 2011 | Annual disclosure within 7 WD of FY end; BOTH Reg 29 (event) and Reg 30 (annual) required |
| 12 | Debt Securities | LODR Reg 52–60; SEBI NCS 2021 | NCRPS (separate from NCD); Commercial Paper (listing within 4 WD); record date 15 days before payment |
| 13 | Capital Actions — Equity | SEBI ICDR 2018; Buyback 2018 | OFS: T-1 intimation, distinct from buyback; preferential/QIP/rights timelines per ICDR chapter |
| 14 | De-listing | SEBI Delisting Regulations 2021; LODR Reg 39 | Entirely separate regulation; PA, SEBI in-principle approval, RBB process, exit pricing — 3–6 month workflow |
| 15 | Investor Grievances / IEPF | LODR Reg 13; Companies Act Sec 124–125 | IEPF transfer after 7 years; advance notice 3 months before transfer; Form IEPF-1 with MCA |
| 16 | ESG / Sustainability | LODR Reg 34(2)(f); SEBI BRSR Circular Jul 2023; SEBI ERP Circular Jan 2023 | BRSR (Top 1000); BRSR Core with assurance (phased Top 150 -> Top 1000); ESG rating change within 24 hours |
| 17 | Cyber Security | SEBI Circular Aug 2023 + LODR Reg 30 | 6-hour reporting for critical incidents (distinct from the 24-hour Reg 30 window) |
| 18 | Large Corporate Framework | SEBI Circular Nov 2018 (amended Oct 2023) | LC identification by 15 April; 25% incremental borrowing via listed debt; 0.25% shortfall penalty |
| 19 | Website / Structural Disclosures | LODR Reg 46; Reg 7(2)–7(3) | MOA/AOA on website; RTA appointment within 7 days; Reg 7(3) half-yearly certificate; post-AGM revised Annual Report within 48 hours |
